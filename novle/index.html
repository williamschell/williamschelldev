Community rules

The gallery is public, and anyone of any age might be reading it. Fiction can go to dark places, but keep it readable for everyone.

  • Post your own writing. Don't copy other people's work.
  • Be kind. No harassment, and don't write to mock real people.
  • Keep it safe for work. No sexual content or graphic gore.
  • Nothing hateful. No slurs or hate speech.
  • No personal info. Don't post anyone's address, phone number or other private details, including your own.

Three reports hide a story until it has been reviewed. Only you can still see your own hidden story, with a "Hidden pending review" label.
